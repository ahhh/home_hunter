# Guest Rules Template

Hipcamp lets hosts add property-specific rules that guests receive with their booking. Copy the block below
into the listing and replace every `[bracket]`. Delete rules that don't apply. Keep the ones marked
**(required)** in some form. They match Hipcamp host standards or Colorado law.

---

## [Property name]: House Rules

**Welcome!** This is a working [ranch / homestead / forest property] and our home. These rules keep you, the land and our neighbors safe.

### Check-in / check-out
- Check-in [3:00-8:00pm]. Check-out by [11:00am]. Please don't arrive after [9:00pm]. Headlights and noise carry.
- Follow the directions and site map we send. GPS isn't reliable here. **Don't drive onto neighbors' property.**

### Occupancy (required)
- Max **[N] people** and **[N] vehicles** per site. No extra tents or guests beyond your booking.
- Visitors who aren't on the booking must be approved in advance.

### Fire (required)
- **Fire restrictions change quickly in Colorado.** We'll message you if a ban starts. **If a fire ban is in effect, no fires of any kind**, even if you had a fire yesterday.
- When fires are allowed: **only in the provided fire ring.** Keep the water bucket and shovel beside it. Never leave a fire unattended. Drown, stir and feel it cold before bed or leaving.
- Don't bring firewood from out of state or far away (it spreads pests). Buy it locally or from us.
- **No fireworks.** No sky lanterns. No smoking in dry grass. Use the ash can.
- Propane stoves: [allowed / allowed only on the picnic table / not allowed during Stage 2].

### Quiet hours (required)
- **10:00pm to 8:00am.** No amplified music at any time. Generators: [not allowed / 8am-8pm only, [N] ft from other sites].

### Toilets and human waste (required)
- Toilet: **[type: flush toilet in bathhouse / vault toilet / portable toilet / bring your own]** at [location].
- [If BYO on 20+ acres:] Bring a self-contained camp toilet or waste bags and **pack all waste out**. No digging catholes [unless permitted: 6-8 in deep, 200 ft from water].
- **RV tanks:** never dump gray or black water on the ground. Nearest dump station: [name, address].

### Water
- [No water is provided; bring what you need] / [Potable water at [location]] / [**Water from the spigot is not for drinking**].
- Dishwater: strain food scraps into the trash and dispose of the water at [location]. Never dump it in the creek.

### Trash (required)
- [Put trash and recycling in the bear-resistant bins at [location]] / [**Pack it in, pack it out**].
- Never leave trash, food or coolers outside or in a tent. **This is bear country.**

### Food and wildlife
- Store food, coolers, toiletries and trash in your **locked vehicle** or [bear box]. Never feed wildlife. Feeding bears or big game is illegal in Colorado.
- Give moose, elk, bears and livestock plenty of room. Close every gate you open.

### Pets (required)
- Pets [allowed / not allowed]. If allowed: **on leash or under voice control at all times**, never left alone, quiet during quiet hours. Pick up after them. Keep them away from livestock and wildlife.

### Parking and roads (required)
- Park only at your site's parking spot. **No parking on the road or shared driveway.**
- Speed limit on our road: **[10] mph**. Neighbors, kids and animals live here.
- [4WD/AWD required Oct-May] / [Road may close in snow or mud].

### Off-limits areas
- Please stay out of: [the house, barns, equipment, livestock pens, the pond/creek, mine openings, the neighbor's land beyond the fence].
- [Public land borders the property at [location]. Its rules and fire orders may differ from ours.]

### Firearms and hunting
- [No discharge of firearms, bows or fireworks on the property.] / [Hunting only with written permission and a CPW license.]
- During hunting season ([dates]), wear blaze orange when walking.

### Hazards on this property (required, per Hipcamp)
- [Ticks / rattlesnakes / steep drop-offs at ___ / fast water in ___ / flash-flood-prone drainage at ___ / lightning above treeline / altitude ([elevation] ft; drink water, watch for altitude sickness) / cold nights, even in summer.]

### Emergencies (required)
- **Call 911.** Property address: **[address]**. GPS: **[lat, lng]**. Site: **[#]**.
- Nearest hospital: [name, address, drive time]. Host phone: [number].
- Evacuation route: [description; map posted at the entrance]. Sign up for alerts: [county alert link].
- Cell service: [best spot]. [Satellite messenger / landline at ___.]

### Leave No Trace
Leave the site better than you found it. Stay on established paths. Don't cut trees or branches. Don't move rocks or build new fire rings.

**By booking, you agree to these rules. Breaking fire, occupancy or quiet-hour rules may mean immediate cancellation without a refund.**
