### Procedures

**Wildfire / smoke**
1. Call 911 if you see an unreported fire.
2. Put out any campfire. Pack up and drive out by the primary route. Headlights on.
3. The host confirms that every site is empty. Tell responders about anyone missing.

**Severe weather** (lightning, hail, high wind, flash flood)
1. Lightning: get into a hard-topped vehicle or a closed building. Stay away from ridges, lone trees and water.
2. Flash flood: move to higher ground right away. Never drive through moving water.
3. High wind: get away from dead trees ("widowmakers"). Collapse tents if it's safe.

**Winter / cold**
1. Hypothermia signs: shivering, confusion, slurred speech. Warm the person and call 911 if severe.
2. If the road is impassable, stay put, run the vehicle only with a clear exhaust pipe, and call the host.

**Medical emergency**
1. Call 911 with the site GPS. 2. Send someone to the entrance to guide responders. 3. Call the host.

**Missing guest**
1. Check the site, vehicle and the usual trails. 2. After [30] minutes with no contact, or right away at night or in bad weather, **call 911.** The sheriff coordinates search and rescue in Colorado. 3. Note the time last seen, clothing, gear, and planned route.

**Wildlife encounter** (bear, lion, moose)
1. Don't run. Make yourself big, back away slowly, and fight back if a bear or lion attacks. 2. Report aggressive wildlife to the host and to Colorado Parks & Wildlife.

**Vehicle stuck / breakdown**
1. Call the host. 2. Don't block the road for emergency vehicles.
