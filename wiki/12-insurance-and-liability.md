# Insurance, Liability & Risk Management

This page is **risk-management information, not a legal opinion on liability.** Talk to a Colorado insurance
broker and attorney before hosting.

## Colorado liability law that matters

| Law | What it does | Hipcamp relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **Premises Liability Act**, [C.R.S. 13-21-115](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/2021/title-13/article-21/part-1/section-13-21-115/) | Sets the landowner's duty by visitor status. **Invitees** (e.g. paying guests) are owed the highest duty: reasonable care against dangers the owner knew or should have known about. | Paying campers are almost certainly **invitees** |
| **Recreational Use Statute**, [C.R.S. 33-41-101 et seq.](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-33/recreational-areas-and-ski-safety/article-41/section-33-41-103/) | Limits liability for owners who let people use land for recreation **"without charge."** | **Doesn't protect paid Hipcamp use.** The charge removes the protection. **Verified** (statute text via Justia). |
| **Agritourism statute**, [C.R.S. 13-21-121](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-13/damages-and-limitations-on-actions/article-21/part-1/section-13-21-121/) | Limits liability for **inherent risks** of agricultural recreation/agritourism, **if** a warning notice is posted or signed | Might apply to a working farm or ranch offering farm-based activities. Whether plain camping counts is uncertain. Ask an attorney. |

### Agritourism warning notice (if it applies)

The statute requires a sign in **black letters at least one inch high**, displayed prominently where the
activities happen, or a signed statement, with this text (**Reported**, from the statute text via Justia/FindLaw):

> UNDER COLORADO LAW, THERE IS NO LIABILITY FOR THE DEATH OF OR INJURY TO A PARTICIPANT IN AN AGRICULTURAL
> RECREATION OR AGRITOURISM ACTIVITY RESULTING FROM THE INHERENT RISKS OF THE AGRICULTURAL RECREATION OR
> AGRITOURISM ACTIVITY, PURSUANT TO SECTION 13-21-121, COLORADO REVISED STATUTES.

A sign isn't a shield against negligence. It covers only **inherent** risks.

## Insurance layers

| Layer | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Hipcamp's host protection** | Hipcamp advertises liability protection for qualifying bookings. Read the current terms: limits, exclusions (structures? activities such as swimming, horses, firearms, ATVs?), whether property damage is covered, and whether it's primary or excess. It covers **Hipcamp bookings only**. |
| **Your homeowners / farm & ranch policy** | Many exclude **business activities** and **paid guests**. Ask the insurer **in writing** whether paid camping is covered or excluded. Not disclosing it can void coverage. |
| **Commercial general liability (CGL)** | Consider it once you have several sites, any structures, off-platform bookings, or activities. Campground-specific policies exist. |
| **Property coverage for guest structures** | Yurts, cabins and bathhouses used commercially may need commercial property coverage. |
| **Umbrella** | Adds limits above the underlying policies. Confirm it isn't excluded for business use. |
| **Workers' comp** | Needed if you hire help (caretaker, cleaner). Colorado requires it for most employers. |

## Risk controls that also help with insurance

- Clear written **guest rules**, accepted at booking ([template](15-guest-rules-template.md))
- Posted **emergency plan** ([template](16-emergency-incident-plan.md))
- Hazard disclosure in the listing (Hipcamp requires it)
- Fire-ban enforcement and daily status checks
- An incident log: date, what happened, who was involved, photos, follow-up
- Inspect sites before each check-in (trees, fire rings, trip hazards)
- Limit or exclude high-risk activities (swimming holes, climbing, ATVs, firearms, horses) unless they're specifically insured
- Consider a liability waiver/release. Colorado courts enforce some releases for recreational activities, but a waiver doesn't cover gross negligence, and drafting matters.

## Questions for your broker

1. Does my current policy exclude paid overnight guests, tents, RVs or structures used by guests?
2. How does Hipcamp's coverage interact with mine: primary, excess, or excluded?
3. What does a small-campground CGL policy cost for [N] sites?
4. Are wildfire-caused losses to neighbors covered if a guest starts a fire?
5. Are the host-provided structures covered for fire, snow load and theft?
