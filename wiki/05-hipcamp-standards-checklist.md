# Hipcamp Host Standards: Compliance Checklist

This page separates three different things:

- **[HC] Hipcamp requirement:** stated in Hipcamp's Host Standards or policies. Hipcamp can delist you for missing these.
- **[CO] Colorado / local law:** comes from the government, whatever Hipcamp says. See the linked page.
- **[BP] Best practice:** recommended, not required.

Sources: [Hipcamp Host Standards](https://www.hipcamp.com/en-US/hoststandards) and
[Hipcamp Toilet Policy](https://host.hipcamp.com/toilet-policy), both read 2026-09-29. **Verified.**
Hipcamp changes these pages without dated versions. Re-check every 12 months.

## Stay safe

| ☐ | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Research and follow local laws and **fire restrictions**. "If a fire ban is in effect, do not allow fires on your property." | HC + CO ([fire](08-fire-and-emergency-safety.md)) |
| ☐ | Tell guests about area **hazards**: ticks, wild animals, poison ivy/oak, drop-offs, strong currents, flood potential, weather risks | HC |
| ☐ | Set a **maximum occupancy** for each site to prevent overcrowding | HC |
| ☐ | Share **emergency contacts**, the **nearest hospital** and **evacuation routes** | HC ([emergency plan](16-emergency-incident-plan.md)) |
| ☐ | **Minimize hazards:** survey for trip/fall risks, keep sites out of flood-prone areas, manage food storage, clear around fire pits, restrict access to dangerous terrain | HC |
| ☐ | Colorado-specific hazards to disclose: lightning, altitude, hypothermia even in summer, afternoon storms, bears/lions, fire-ban status, winter road closures | BP |

## Be a good neighbor

| ☐ | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Tell neighbors** about hosting, especially where roads are shared, traffic increases or campsites are visible | HC |
| ☐ | **Garbage:** tell guests exactly where trash, recycling and compost go, or that they must pack it out | HC |
| ☐ | **Off-street parking for all guests.** Post speed limits and remind guests that neighbors live here. | HC ([access](13-access-parking.md)) |
| ☐ | **Toilet** that meets the toilet policy (see below) | HC + CO ([toilets](06-toilets-septic-waste.md)) |
| ☐ | **Quiet hours 10:00pm-8:00am**, unless your area has other established quiet hours | HC |
| ☐ | **Signage:** public-facing signs only as local law allows; onsite signs encouraged | HC + CO |
| ☐ | **Pets:** guests keep pets under control, prevent harm, keep them quiet during quiet hours | HC |
| ☐ | Bear-resistant trash storage where bears are active | BP (some towns require it) |

### Toilet policy in detail

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| All campsites must have **access to a toilet**, provided by the Host or the Hipcamper. | Toilet Policy |
| A **permitted toilet** (indoor, porta-potty, etc.) is **required** when hosting **tent campers on properties under 20 acres**. | Host Standards |
| On **20+ acres**, or for **dry camping by self-contained RVs only**, a host toilet isn't required. Guests then must bring their own camp toilet. | Host Standards / Toilet Policy |
| Listings with **sleeping accommodations** (anything you provide to sleep in) must offer **Tier B-E**. **Tier A is prohibited.** | Toilet Policy |
| Toilets should ideally be **no more than 55 yards (50 m)** from each site or cabin. | Toilet Policy |
| Host-provided toilets must be **onsite, with unrestricted access** for guests. | Toilet Policy |
| The listing must **say which toilet type** is offered. | Toilet Policy |

Toilet tiers: **A** primitive/portable camp toilet · **B** self-contained RV toilet · **C** contracted, serviced
portable toilet · **D** enclosed structure with a toilet, cleaned · **E** enclosed structure with a toilet, cleaned, plus hand-washing.

> **Colorado check:** a "permitted" toilet here means one the local public health agency allows: a
> connection to a permitted OWTS, a permitted vault privy or composting toilet, or a serviced portable toilet.
> Some counties (Lake County, for example) push back on vault/composting toilets for campgrounds. See [Toilets](06-toilets-septic-waste.md).

## Be a good citizen

| ☐ | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Income tax:** Hipcamp issues 1099s to hosts above the federal reporting thresholds (Hipcamp cites $20,000 and 200 bookings). All income is reportable either way. | HC + federal |
| ☐ | **Occupancy/lodging tax:** may apply. In Colorado, Hipcamp collects and remits state and state-administered taxes for bookings after Sept 9, 2024. Hosts may still owe **home-rule city** taxes. | HC + CO ([taxes](10-taxes-and-licensing.md)) |
| ☐ | **Business license** fees may apply | HC + CO |
| ☐ | **Local law compliance:** "There may be state, county, or local laws that regulate or even prohibit hosting Hipcampers on your property." | HC + CO ([zoning](02-jurisdiction-and-zoning.md)) |

## Be a respectful host

| ☐ | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Reply to booking requests and inquiries **within 24 hours** | HC |
| ☐ | Stay in contact before and during the stay, especially about arrival | HC |
| ☐ | Keep the calendar current; use calendar sync; accept available dates | HC |
| ☐ | **Listing accuracy:** detailed directions, photos that show the actual site, an up-to-date description | HC |
| ☐ | **Inclusion:** safe and welcoming regardless of identity; no hate | HC |
| ☐ | **Honor all bookings.** Unwarranted cancellations hurt search ranking and can lead to deactivation. | HC |
| ☐ | GPS pin checked on the ground; offline directions (Colorado has large dead zones) | BP |

## Listing policies

| ☐ | Item | Type |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Not a home rental** in a suburban/residential area. Hipcamp is about nature access. | HC |
| ☐ | **No one-time events or festivals.** The land must stay continuously available. | HC |
| ☐ | **Residential neighborhoods** are generally unsuitable unless on substantial acreage | HC |
| ☐ | HOA or land-trust permission obtained if required | HC mentions + CO ([property rights](11-property-rights-hoas-easements.md)) |
