# Environmental & Site Constraints

Checklist of Colorado site constraints. Each row says **which agency to ask**. Sites differ too much for this
page to give answers.

| ☐ | Constraint | How to check | Who decides / further review |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | **Floodplain** | FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer (msc.fema.gov); county floodplain maps; Colorado Hazard Mapping (CWCB) | County floodplain administrator: floodplain development permit. Hipcamp also says to keep sites out of flood-prone areas. |
| ☐ | **Streams, wetlands, riparian areas** | National Wetlands Inventory; site visit | U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (Clean Water Act §404) for any fill; county setbacks from water; OWTS setbacks from water |
| ☐ | **Wildfire hazard** | Colorado Forest Atlas / Wildfire Risk Public Viewer | Fire district; Wildfire Resiliency Code for structures ([fire](08-fire-and-emergency-safety.md)) |
| ☐ | **Steep slopes / geologic hazards** (rockfall, landslide, expansive soils, mine subsidence) | Colorado Geological Survey maps; county hazard overlays | County (often a geologic hazard review); CGS |
| ☐ | **Wildlife habitat and migration corridors** | CPW species activity mapping (SAM); county habitat overlays | County (e.g. [Chaffee](counties/chaffee.md) closes commercial campsites in the most sensitive season inside key production/winter habitat; [Gunnison](counties/gunnison.md) requires sage-grouse review for long-term camping); CPW consults |
| ☐ | **Bears and lions** | CPW; local ordinances | Bear-resistant trash and food storage is required in some municipalities and HOAs, and recommended everywhere in bear country. **Feeding big game or bears is prohibited** in Colorado. |
| ☐ | **Protected / sensitive species** | CPW; U.S. Fish & Wildlife IPaC | USFWS for federally listed species |
| ☐ | **1041 areas** (areas/activities of state interest) | County 1041 regulations | County 1041 permit |
| ☐ | **Cultural / historic resources** | County; History Colorado | County historic review, where adopted |
| ☐ | **Noxious weeds** | County weed list | **Colorado Noxious Weed Act** ([C.R.S. 35-5.5](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-35/pest-and-weed-control/article-5-5/)): landowners must manage listed noxious weeds. Guest traffic spreads seeds, so clean vehicles and control weeds at sites. |
| ☐ | **Erosion / drainage** | Site visit after rain | County grading/erosion permits for significant disturbance; CDPHE stormwater permit if ≥1 acre is disturbed |
| ☐ | **Dark sky** | County/town lighting code | Custer County (Westcliffe/Silver Cliff are an International Dark Sky community) and others restrict outdoor lighting. Use shielded, downward, warm lights. |
| ☐ | **Scenic byways / viewsheds** | County overlay | Chaffee's 2022 resolution scored sites within 300 yards of a scenic byway for major review. [Routt](counties/routt.md) requires shielding commercial campsites from public roads. |
| ☐ | **Adjacent public land** | BLM/USFS maps | Guests may cross over, so post the boundary. Fire orders on public land may differ from yours. |
| ☐ | **Hunting season** | CPW season dates; your own/neighbor leases | Orange-clothing notices; no-shooting zones; think about closing during rifle seasons |
| ☐ | **Mining legacy** (open shafts, tailings) | CGS; Colorado Division of Reclamation, Mining & Safety | Fence or avoid; disclose as a hazard (Hipcamp requires hazard disclosure) |

## Leave No Trace baseline

Whatever the regulations say, Colorado landscapes (alpine tundra, desert crusts, riparian zones) recover
slowly. Use established sites, durable surfaces and designated paths. Keep campsites **200 ft from water**
where possible (Leave No Trace guidance, and often a county or OWTS setback too).
